Here's the honest pitch, from us to you, like a friend telling you about something worth your time.

We built Sual because we were tired of the same problem every serious student of knowledge knows too well: you want to learn Fiqh, Seerah, Arabic, and the classical sciences properly — with sources, with structure — and what you find online is either a five-minute reel or a forum thread with no citation in sight. So we built the thing we wished existed.

Here's what's waiting for you inside:

📚 Seven disciplines, properly levelled. Fiqh, Seerah, Arabiyyah, Usul al-Fiqh, Sarf, Nahw, and Tafseer — each one Beginner to Advanced, every answer sourced.
🎯 Quizzes that actually track you. Take one, see your score, watch your level climb — for real, not just a tap through a menu.
🧠 The Hifdh Simulator — our favourite thing we've built. Guard your memorization of the Qur'an, the Arba'in, or all 430 hadith of Umdatul-Ahkam, with drills built for how hifdh actually fades, not just flashcards.
🕋 Prayer times, Hijri calendar, duas, flashcards, seerah stories, and tajweed — the everyday tools, all in one place, all free.
🎓 Spaces — for those who want more: scholar-answered discussions, and live weekly Arabiyyah and Hadeeth classes that go all the way to the Alfiyyah and Sahih al-Bukhari.

We're three days out from opening the doors. We'd genuinely love for you to be there from the first moment. Come join us where we'll be sharing it all live. Also, don't forget to tell your friends about it!
