Getting Started — Five Minutes to Your First Win

1 Create your account. Head to app.usesual.com and sign up with your email. It takes under a minute.
2 Choose your level. Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced — be honest with yourself here. You can always move up as you grow; the content is matched to meet you where you are.
3 Pick a discipline and read a few Q&As. Fiqh, Seerah, Arabiyyah, Usul al-Fiqh, Sarf, Nahw, or Tafseer — every answer is sourced, so you're never just taking our word for it.
4 Take your first quiz. This is where it becomes real — your score, your average, your level, all tracked from this very first attempt.
5 Open the Hifdh Simulator. If you're memorizing Qur'an, the Arba'in, or Umdatul-Ahkam, tell it how far your hifdh reaches and let it start reviewing with you.

Put Sual on Your Home Screen

Sual works right in your browser, but it's built to feel like a real app on your phone too — one tap from your home screen, no app store needed.

On iPhone (Safari)

Open app.usesual.com in Safari, tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen, then scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."

On Android (Chrome)

Open app.usesual.com in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu in the top corner, then tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app."
